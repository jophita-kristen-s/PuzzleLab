# PuzzleLab project notes

## What to demonstrate in a portfolio review

- Explain why Sudoku is a constraint-satisfaction problem.
- Walk through a candidate placement and a backtrack.
- Describe the distinction between candidate attempts and backtracks.
- Explain the worst-case nature of backtracking and why pruning matters.
- Discuss limitations: fixed sample puzzles, no backend, and browser-dependent benchmark timing.
- Show how you would test `isValid`, conflict detection, and whether the solver preserves the original clues.

## Example resume bullet (edit to reflect your own work)

Built a responsive Sudoku learning interface with a JavaScript backtracking solver, step-by-step decision replay, constraint validation, and runtime/search metrics.

Only claim features you have personally verified and can explain.
